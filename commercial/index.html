Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Bedford HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan service around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts and the heat fast. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Bedford

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Bedford HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Bedford and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. Our first priority is pinpointing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. After that, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.
